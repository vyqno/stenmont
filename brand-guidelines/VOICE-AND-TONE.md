# Voice and tone

Write as a capable financial operations colleague: direct, precise and calm. Lead with the business action and its consequence. Use the same name for an entity across UI, docs, email and exports.

| Situation | Write | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Connect your site to accept payments. | Unlock limitless commerce. |
| Pending | Payment submitted. We are checking confirmation. | Paid! before confirmation |
| Confirmed | Payment confirmed. View receipt. | Instant finality guaranteed. |
| Failure | We could not confirm this payment. Check its status before retrying. | Something magical went wrong. |
| Approval | Approve a refund of 12.50 USDT for invoice INV-104. | Approve this action. |
| Empty state | No invoices yet. Create your first invoice. | Your journey starts here. |

Merchant language explains costs, limits and permissions. Customer primary flows explain amount, business, receipt and next step. Developer reference uses exact protocol names, units, errors and environment boundaries. Existing-wallet instructions may name wallets where necessary to complete that chosen task.

Never describe World human proof as KYC, ENS policy metadata as enforced spending limits, or testnet activity as commercial traction. Give the source and date for performance claims. Avoid guarantees, vague superlatives and “revolution,” “disrupt,” or “next-generation Web3.”
