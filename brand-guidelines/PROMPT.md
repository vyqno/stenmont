# Vector asset brief

This file supersedes the earlier Whitechain-colour image prompts for new work. The existing application remains on legacy tokens until a separate migration. See COLORS.md.

Use the approved Paper White #FFFFFF, Ink #050505, Cornflower #548CFF and Neutral Grey #E8E9EC palette. Functional colours are permitted only for their documented status. No gradients, dither, glow, 3D objects or cryptocurrency decoration.

Draw explanatory vectors on a grid: 2 px illustration strokes; 1.5 px icon strokes on a 24 px grid. Use aligned record columns and event rails, generous whitespace and limited emphasis. Explain a confirmed product concept; do not invent bank settlement, automatic compliance or enforced agent spending from metadata.

Never generate or retype the wordmark. Request the founder's custom master. Do not request All Round Gothic text as a replacement. Never embed font binaries. Neue Haas Grotesk typography requires licence and weight verification before use.

Label conceptual visuals explicitly. Use actual product captures only after a real run, without keys, personal data or unapproved third-party imagery. Do not imitate any company's composition, photography or copy.

## Required future assets

Founder-approved custom master, Ink and reversed White variants, monochrome reproduction and approved favicon. Until supplied, reserve placement space and label it as awaiting artwork; do not fabricate a branded mark.
