## Placement specification

Pending final artwork testing, use clear space of at least one lowercase t-height on every side. Measure from the visible artwork, not its SVG bounding box. Start at 16 px visible height on screen or 5 mm in print; these are provisional production minima and require legibility checks against the actual master.

Use Ink on Paper White and Paper White on Ink. A flat Neutral Grey background may carry the Ink mark. Keep the mark out of a busy image. Cornflower backgrounds require a reviewed Ink export; never automatically reverse it to white.

## Incorrect use

Never uppercase, retype, stretch, compress, redraw, outline, animate individual letters, add a symbol, apply gradients/shadows, or recolour individual characters. Do not substitute a generic rounded font. Do not use third-party framework icons as the Stenmont favicon.

## Export handoff

Ask the founder for original SVG/PDF artwork with explicit reproduction rights. Check viewBox, tight bounds, path integrity and monochrome output at the minimum size. Export only from that master; retain it unchanged. `assets/logo/` is reserved for these approved files.
