# Brand strategy

Status: founder brief translated into operating rules, 6 November 2026. Visual implementation in the app is deferred.

## Offer and audience

Stenmont builds commerce infrastructure for businesses and the developers who connect it: payment orchestration, USDT settlement, merchant records, invoice financing and controlled agent access. The merchant earns; the payer brings their own USDT. Merchant setup should require no crypto expertise.

The buying moment is a business connecting its existing sales channel and needing reliable records after payment. Developers need exact interfaces; finance teams need amounts, dates and evidence; customers need to know what they are paying and what happened.

## Promise and proof

Make payment, settlement and records understandable in one business workflow. Support every claim with the actual integration and its limitations. The current deployment is staging; it is not evidence of mainnet availability, regulatory approval or institutional adoption. Pricing is undecided.

| Attribute | Observable design rule |
|---|---|
| Credible | Name the environment and distinguish pending from confirmed |
| Precise | Align amounts, label units, keep identifiers copyable |
| Clear | One primary task per section, descriptive verbs |
| Durable | Use a grid and restrained hierarchy, not visual trends |
| Technical | Give developers reproducible examples and explicit failures |

## Distinctive construction rule

Use an aligned record column and a horizontal event rail: each stage has a label, evidence and a state. This comes from the product's invoice → payment → record behavior. Use it for explanatory diagrams, never to suggest unbuilt automation or an approved new logo.

## Founder decisions

Preserve the custom lowercase wordmark. Supporting type is Neue Haas Grotesk. Core colours are Paper White, Ink, Cornflower and Neutral Grey. No speculative crypto imagery. No copied company composition, imagery or copy.

## Open decisions

Approved wordmark vector exports, favicon and licence evidence are missing from the permitted asset inventory. Final tagline and pricing need founder approval. No new logo, fee claim, certification or trademark clearance is implied by this book.
