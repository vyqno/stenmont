# Messaging

## Message hierarchy

1. What it does: infrastructure for accepting payments and managing the records that follow.
2. How a business starts: connect its site through developer tools or a scoped agent connection.
3. What happens afterward: invoices, refunds, receipts and exports in the same workflow.
4. Advanced capabilities: financing and agent controls with their actual requirements stated.

## Working copy — not approved taglines

- Commerce infrastructure, connected.
- Payments and settlement. One integration.
- Built for modern commerce.

Example explainer: “Connect Stenmont to accept USDT payments and track invoices, refunds and receipts.” Name the staging environment in demo material. Do not imply published package availability until publishing has actually happened.

## Evidence and boundaries

Use “payment submitted” before confirmation, “payment confirmed” after verified confirmation, and “settled claim” only after the financing lifecycle says so. “Gas covered” requires naming who pays and the applicable limits. Do not promise a lower financing discount until the buyer-acceptance pricing rule exists and is applied.

Pricing, headline tagline, mainnet launch claims and institutional endorsements remain founder decisions. No competitor's copy, composition or imagery is a source asset for this system.
